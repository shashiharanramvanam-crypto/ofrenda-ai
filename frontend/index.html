🕊️ Why Open Innovation Matters for Grief & Memory

1. Sacred Privacy & Data Sovereignty:
Grief, family remembrances, and ancestral photographs are the most vulnerable, intimate data a human being can possess. Placing them into a closed commercial cloud API means subjecting your loved one's memory to corporate data scraping, telemetry logging, and monetization. With local open-weight Gemma, inference occurs entirely on your device's RAM. Not a single byte leaves your machine.


2. Immunity to Cloud Rot & Digital Extinction:
Closed platforms suffer from "cloud rot" — startups fold, proprietary APIs are deprecated, subscriptions lapse, and services terminate accounts. A memorial altar should not vanish because an API vendor updated their pricing. Ofrenda AI runs on open weights and plain JSON/HTML files that will run 30 years from now on any computer without an internet connection.


3. True Offline Remembrance (With the Lights & Wi-Fi Off):
Traditional Día de los Muertos is celebrated in quiet rooms lit only by candle flames. You can take this application to a remote cemetery, an off-grid family cabin, or a darkened sanctuary with Wi-Fi switched off. The ghost in the machine answers from the local silicon.
